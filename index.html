01 — Background

Building Secure
Intelligent Systems

Advancing trustworthy artificial intelligence through deep learning, security, privacy, and human-centered computing.

I am Dabbrata Das, a Ph.D. applicant and researcher specializing in deep learning, intelligent systems, security, and privacy. I currently serve as a Lecturer at Uttara University, Dhaka, Bangladesh, and am affiliated with the Data Agency and Security (DAS) Lab at George Mason University.

I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science in 2023, graduating with a CGPA of 3.64 and ranking 38th in my department. My research experience spans Computer Vision, Image Processing, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning, with an emphasis on developing robust, secure, and trustworthy intelligent systems.

Beyond core AI research, my interests extend to Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), healthcare robotics, cybersecurity, and privacy-preserving intelligent technologies. I am passionate about designing human-centered AI solutions that address real-world challenges and actively pursue collaborative research and scientific publications.
